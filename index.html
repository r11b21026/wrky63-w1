VMD Web Touch & Controls Manual

Mobile / Touch Gestures

  • 1 Finger Drag: Rotate molecular model in 3D
  • 2 Finger Pinch: Zoom In / Zoom Out
  • 2 Finger Drag: Pan / Translate structure across view

Desktop Mouse & Keyboard

  • Left Drag: Rotate
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom
  • Right Drag: Pan
  • Spacebar: Play / Pause trajectory

Poster Presentation Tip (QR Code)

In academic poster sessions, upload your representative Multi-model PDB (or stride-reduced trajectory < 3MB) to GitHub Pages. Attendees scanning your QR code get this exact VMD visual experience directly on their phones with zero app installation.
